Gaming Platforms

PC Gaming refers to playing video games on a personal computer instead of a console or mobile device. It is one of the most powerful and flexible gaming platforms available today. PC gamers use desktops or laptops with dedicated graphics cards, powerful processors, and high-speed memory to run games smoothly. Unlike consoles, PCs can be upgraded — players can change the graphics card, increase RAM, or upgrade storage to improve performance.
